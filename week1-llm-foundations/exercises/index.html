LLM Foundations

An LLM is a non-deterministic, token-priced next-token predictor. You can't assertEqual its output, so you version its inputs like code, and measure its cost and behaviour like an SRE.

Two things that will trip you up

  • A response is a list of blocks. Haiku 5.5 thinks by default, so the first block may be thinking. Every lab picks text blocks by type.
  • max_tokens covers thinking and the answer. Set it too low and you can get thinking with no answer.
